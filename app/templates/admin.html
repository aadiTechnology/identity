Import people from Project Tracker

Brings Project Tracker's companies and people into Identity with their existing passwords, so the same sign-in works here. Nobody becomes an Identity administrator. Step 1: list the companies. Step 2: tick the ones to bring across and preview (writes nothing). Step 3: import.

Service clients

How a product's server gets a short-lived token to call a shared service (such as the Video Engine) for one company. A company must be subscribed to both the calling product and the service; turn the service on for a company with its chip above. A secret is shown once.

Plans

A plan is a package of one product: the features it includes and the limits that apply (leave a limit empty for unlimited). Products read the plan of each company from Identity, so a package can change without changing the product. A company with no plan keeps today's behaviour.
